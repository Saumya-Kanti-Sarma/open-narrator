## 🔍 What is Open Narrator?

Open Narrator is a **free, local-first AI voice generator and content creation tool** that converts text into realistic AI-generated voice and transforms that voice into **publish-ready content** such as:

* YouTube videos (short & long)
* Instagram Reels / TikTok videos
* Podcasts
* Audiobooks
* Story narration
* Educational content

It is designed for **content creators, developers, and students** who want **unlimited AI voice generation without cloud dependency**.
